1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with TulusSG, we may collect information such as:

  • your name;
  • mobile or WhatsApp number;
  • email address, where provided;
  • nationality;
  • preferred language;
  • information you choose to share with us about your experiences, interests or circumstances;
  • event or programme registration information;
  • information relating to your participation in TulusSG activities;
  • communications you send to us; and
  • basic technical information generated when you use our website.

We only ask for information that is reasonably required for the relevant TulusSG activity, service, programme or enquiry.

2. Why we collect your information

We may use your personal information to:

  • respond to your enquiries;
  • process registrations and expressions of interest;
  • contact you about TulusSG programmes, events or opportunities you have expressed interest in;
  • administer community activities and participation;
  • communicate important information relating to your registration or participation;
  • understand the needs and interests of the communities we work with;
  • improve TulusSG programmes, services and digital platforms;
  • maintain appropriate records for operational, safety or administrative purposes; and
  • comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

We will not use your personal information for purposes that are materially different from those for which it was collected without an appropriate basis or further consent where required.

3. Share Your Story campaign

If you register your interest in the TulusSG Share Your Story project, we may collect:

  • your name;
  • WhatsApp or mobile number;
  • nationality;
  • preferred language;
  • the story or information you choose to share with us (optional);
  • the social media accounts you choose to share, if any;
  • your consent for TulusSG to contact you about the opportunity; and
  • your media consent (see below).

Media consent. To register, you must agree that, if you are selected and take part, TulusSG may photograph, film and record you, and use your image, voice and story in the Share Your Story production. The production may be edited and published on TulusSG’s website and on TulusSG’s Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and official WhatsApp channels, for the Share Your Story campaign. We record that you agreed, when, and the version of the wording you agreed to.

Registering does not mean you will be selected. If you are selected, we will explain the production to you before any filming, photography or recording.

If you have concerns about showing your face or using your actual voice, please email admin@tulussg.com before submitting the form. We can discuss possible ways for you to participate.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing admin@tulussg.com. If you withdraw before filming, you will not take part in the production. If you withdraw later, we will stop using your image, voice and story in new materials, but we may not be able to remove them from materials already published or shared.

4. Sensitive or personal stories

Some people may choose to share personal experiences with TulusSG.

You should only provide information that you are comfortable sharing with us.

Please avoid submitting unnecessary confidential information such as:

  • passport numbers;
  • FIN numbers;
  • bank account information;
  • passwords;
  • detailed residential addresses; or
  • other information that is not required for the relevant TulusSG activity.

Where personal stories are provided to us, we will treat them with appropriate care and use them only for the purpose for which they were submitted, unless further permission is obtained.

5. Who may access your information

Personal information may be accessed by authorised persons who require it for legitimate TulusSG purposes, which may include:

  • members of the TulusSG team;
  • authorised administrative personnel;
  • technology or service providers supporting TulusSG systems;
  • programme partners where this is necessary for the relevant activity and appropriate safeguards are in place; and
  • professional advisers where reasonably necessary.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not publish your personal information merely because you submitted a registration or expression of interest.

6. Service providers

TulusSG may use trusted technology providers to operate its website, forms, email, storage, communications and other digital services.

For example, registration information may be processed through secure website infrastructure and stored using services such as private cloud databases or private Google Workspace tools.

These providers may process information on our behalf only as necessary to provide the relevant service.

7. How long we keep information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including operational, administrative, legal and record-keeping requirements.

For expressions of interest that do not progress further, we aim to remove or anonymise information when it is no longer reasonably required.

Information relating to ongoing participants, programmes or legitimate operational records may be retained for a longer period where necessary.

8. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information against:

  • unauthorised access;
  • misuse;
  • loss;
  • disclosure; and
  • inappropriate alteration.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your choices

You may contact us if you wish to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • withdraw from a programme or expression of interest;
  • withdraw consent for future contact;
  • request deletion of information where appropriate; or
  • raise a concern about how your information has been handled.

Where consent is withdrawn, this will not affect actions already lawfully taken before the withdrawal.

Certain information may need to be retained where required for legal, administrative, safety or legitimate record-keeping purposes.

10. Children and young persons

TulusSG does not intend to collect personal information from children through forms designed for adult participants unless the relevant programme specifically involves children and appropriate consent arrangements have been established.

If you believe that personal information relating to a child has been submitted to us inappropriately, please contact us.

11. Photos, videos and media

When you join TulusSG, or take part in a TulusSG programme, activity or event, you agree that TulusSG may take photos and videos of you, record interviews, and use your image, voice and story for TulusSG events, activities, publicity, social media and media productions.

We will let you know when photography, filming or interviews are taking place.

If you do not want your image, voice or story to be used, or you change your mind later, please tell us at admin@tulussg.com. We will stop using it in new materials. We may not be able to remove it from materials that have already been published or shared.

For media productions that you register for, such as Share Your Story, you give this consent on the registration form. Section 3 explains what it covers and how to withdraw it.

12. External links

The TulusSG website may contain links to websites, platforms or services operated by other organisations.

Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and TulusSG is not responsible for how those third-party services handle information.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as TulusSG develops its services, programmes or digital systems.

The latest version will be published on the TulusSG website with its updated date.
