### Section 3 - Share Your Story campaigns
- (Intro) TulusSG runs two Share Your Story campaigns: **Share Your Story: Foreign Domestic Workers** (registration open from 15 October to 15 November 2026) and **Share Your Story: Foreign Workers** (registration open from 26 October to 26 November 2026). Both work in the same way, in two stages. Each campaign's registrations are kept in a spreadsheet set aside separately for it.

**Stage 1: Registering your interest**
- If you fill in the registration form, we collect the following: your name, WhatsApp number, nationality, preferred language; the story you choose to share (if any); the social media accounts you choose to share (if any); your email address, only if you ask for a copy of your form; and your consent - together with the date and time, the version of the wording you agreed to, and the language the page was shown in. We also give your registration a registration number.
- We use this information to consider your submission, to understand who is taking part in the campaign, and to contact you about the campaign you registered for. Unless you separately agree, we will not use these for marketing or for other campaigns.
- Only people authorised by TulusSG to manage the campaign, and the service providers who run our registration system, can see your registration (see section 6). If your story contains personal or sensitive experiences, we handle it with special care (see section 4).
- During selection, we may speak with you by WhatsApp or telephone to learn more about your story. These conversations are not audio-video recorded for publication; you do not need to sign anything to take part in them.
- Registering does not mean you will be selected. Registration does not give us permission to film you, photograph you, audio-video record you, or publish your story or personal information.

**Stage 2: If you are selected**
- We will explain to you the planned production. Before filming, photographing, audio or video recording, or any interview held for media production or publication, we will ask you separately for written permission. That written permission will explain how your story, name, image, voice and audio-video recordings may be used; it also includes how you wish to be identified, the planned publication platforms, whether the content may be available indefinitely, and the arrangements for withdrawing permission.
- People or organisations doing production work for TulusSG will be given only the personal information reasonably necessary for their agreed work.
- **Copy of your form.** If you ask for a copy, we will first email a link to the address you give, to check that the address is yours. We use that address only to send the copy.

**Contact form**
- If you write to us through the contact form on our home page, we receive your name, email address, the role you choose, and your message. We use them to reply to you and to follow up on what you asked about - for example, volunteering or working with us. We do not add them to the campaign registrations; we do not use them for marketing either. Section 7 explains how long we keep them.

When that happens, we rely on the contracts we have with these providers, which contain data protection and information security obligations, to ensure the information is protected to a standard comparable to the protection under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act.

## Changes made

1. Block 54 heading and text, block 53 item 3 (section 9). "Withdraw from a campaign". Before: ஒரு திட்டத்திலிருந்து உங்கள் பதிவைத் திரும்பப் பெற ("withdraw your registration from a campaign"). After: ஒரு திட்டத்திலிருந்து விலக / விலகுதல் / நீங்கள் விலகினால் ("leave / withdraw from a campaign"). Reason: the English right is to stop participating at any stage. "Withdraw your registration" suggests it only applies before selection, and it reuses திரும்பப் பெறு, the same verb used for withdrawing consent in the next paragraph. விலகு keeps participation and consent clearly separate.
2. Block 19 (section 3, Stage 1). "These conversations are not recorded for publication". Before: ஒலி-ஒளிப்பதிவு செய்யப்படுவதில்லை (could be read as "not audio-visually recorded"). After: ஒலிப்பதிவோ காணொளிப் பதிவோ செய்யப்படுவதில்லை ("neither audio-recorded nor video-recorded"). Reason: these are phone/WhatsApp calls. A reader could take "audio-visual" to mean an audio-only recording is still allowed. The promise needs to cover audio explicitly.
3. Block 44, table row 5 (section 7). "Your working records". Before: உங்கள் நடைமுறைப் பதிவேடுகள் ("your procedural/practical records", unclear). After: திட்டப் பணிக்காக நாங்கள் வைத்திருக்கும் உங்கள் பதிவேடுகள் ("your records we keep for the campaign work"). Reason: the original label is hard to understand for readers with modest literacy, and it decides which records are deleted within 30 days.
4. Block 38 (section 6). "Private Google Sheets". Before: பொதுவில் பகிரப்படாத ("not publicly shared"). After: பொதுமக்கள் அணுக முடியாத, தனிப்பட்ட (private) ("not accessible to the public, private"). Reason: "not publicly shared" is weaker than "private" and could suggest the sheets are shared with others.
5. Block 70 (section 12A). "The registration form records the language". Before: பதிவுப் படிவம் பதிவு செய்கிறது (பதிவு used both as "registration" and as "record" in the same phrase). After: பதிவுப் படிவம் குறித்துக்கொள்கிறது ("notes down"). Reason: keeps பதிவு meaning only "registration", as the translator intended.
