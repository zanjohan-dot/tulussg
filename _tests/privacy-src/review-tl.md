### Introduction
- TulusSG is committed to the responsible and respectful handling of personal information.
- TulusSG is a ground-up community initiative of **Zan J Private Limited (UEN: 202616105R)**. TulusSG runs the Share Your Story campaigns.
- Zan J Private Limited, which operates TulusSG, is the one responsible for collecting and using the personal information you provide. In this Privacy Policy, "we", "our/us" and "TulusSG" refer to Zan J Private Limited acting through its initiatives and brands.
- This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have.

### 3. Share Your Story campaigns
TulusSG has two Share Your Story campaigns: **Share Your Story: Migrant Domestic Workers** (registration open from 15 October to 15 November 2026) and **Share Your Story: Migrant Workers** (registration open from 26 October to 26 November 2026). Both work the same way, in two stages. Each campaign's registrations are stored in its own separate spreadsheet.

**Stage 1: Registering your interest**
- If you fill in the registration form, we collect: your name, WhatsApp number, nationality and preferred language; the story you chose to share, if any; the social media accounts you chose to share, if any; your email address, only if you ask for a copy of your form; and your consent, including the date and time, the version of the wording you agreed to, and the language in which the page was shown. We also give your registration a reference number.
- We use this information to review your submission, understand the profile of participants in the campaign, and contact you about the campaign you registered for. We do not use it for marketing or for another campaign unless you separately agree.
- Your registration can be seen only by people authorised by TulusSG to manage the campaign, and by the service providers that run our registration system (see section 6). If your story includes personal or sensitive experiences, we take extra care of it (see section 4).
- During selection, we may talk to you on WhatsApp or by phone to get to know your story better. These conversations are not recorded for publication, and you do not need to sign anything to take part in them.
- Registering does not mean you will be selected. Nor does it allow us to film you, photograph you or record you, or publish your story or personal information.

**Stage 2: If you are selected**
- We will explain the proposed production. We will ask for your separate written permission before any filming, photography, audio or video recording, or any interview intended for media production or publication. The written permission will explain how your story, name, image, voice and recordings may be used, including how you want to be identified, the channels where it is planned to be published, whether the content may remain available indefinitely, and the rules on withdrawing permission.
- People or organisations working on the production for TulusSG will receive only the personal information reasonably needed for the agreed work.
- **Copy of your form.** If you ask for a copy, we will first email a link to the address you give, to make sure it is yours. We use that address only to send the copy.

**Contact form**
If you write to us using the contact form on our home page, we receive your name, email address, the role you chose and your message. We use these to reply to you and to follow up on what you asked about, for example volunteering or working with us. We do not combine them with campaign registrations or use them for marketing. Section 7 explains how long we keep them.

### 6. Service providers and processing overseas
- We use service providers to run our website, registration system and communications. They process personal information only according to our instructions.
- Providers that hold information you give us:
  - **Google (Google Workspace and Google Cloud).** Our registration server runs on Google Cloud in Singapore. Registrations are stored in private Google Sheets, a separate one for each campaign, and our emails, including the copy of your form if you ask for it, are sent and stored in Gmail.
  - **Google Apps Script.** It delivers to our email the messages sent using the contact form on our home page.
  - **WhatsApp (Meta).** If we talk to you on WhatsApp, the conversation is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms.
- Providers that only see that you visited our website: **GitHub**, which hosts the website, and **Google Fonts**, which provides the letter fonts. Like any website, they receive technical information such as your device's IP address. They do not receive your form answers. YouTube is covered in section 12A.
- Some of this information may be stored or processed outside Singapore, in the providers' data centres. When this happens, we rely on our agreements with these providers, which include data protection and security obligations, to ensure the information receives a standard of protection comparable to that under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act.
- We remain responsible for the personal information our providers process for us.

### 9. Your choices
You may contact us at admin@tulussg.com to:
- ask for access to the personal information we hold about you, and how it has been used or disclosed in the past year;
- ask us to correct wrong information;
- withdraw from a campaign, or ask us to stop contacting you;
- withdraw your consent to our use of your personal information, or a permission you gave;
- ask us to delete your personal information; or
- raise a concern about how we handle your information.

- **Withdrawing from a campaign.** If you withdraw from a campaign, we will stop your participation and related communications. Your personal information will be handled according to the retention and deletion rules described in section 7.
- **Withdrawing consent.** We tell you what it means for you, for example that you can no longer take part, and we stop collecting, using and disclosing your personal information for that purpose, unless the law requires or allows otherwise. Withdrawal does not affect what was already done before your request.
- **Deletion.** You may ask for your personal information to be deleted. We will review and act on your request in line with applicable law and our retention policy. If some information must be kept, we will explain what is kept and why.
- If your request is about photos, recordings or published media, we will also explain how it affects any separate media agreement you signed. A media agreement does not remove your rights under data protection law.
- You do not need your registration reference, but it helps us find your information. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act on a request. We try to confirm we received requests within five working days and to reply as soon as reasonably possible. If we cannot reply to a request for access or correction within 30 days, we will tell you in writing when we expect to be able to reply. Usually we do not charge for privacy-related requests; if the law allows a fee and we intend to charge it, we will tell you the amount before we proceed.

### 11. Photos, videos and media
- **TulusSG events and activities.** We may take photos and videos at our events and activities to show what the community does, for example on our website and social media. We will let you know when photos or video are being taken. If you have a concern about a photo or recording of you, please contact us at admin@tulussg.com. We will review your request and act according to the law, any permission you gave, and the circumstances of the photo or recording. When you withdraw consent that we rely on, we will follow our legal obligations, including stopping further use if the law requires it.
- **Media productions, such as Share Your Story.** These are different. For a media production, we will film or photograph you, record or interview you only if you have separate written permission. That permission states how the content may be used and how withdrawal works. Section 3 explains the process.

### 14. Languages
- Our pages are written in English and translated into Bahasa Indonesia, Filipino, Burmese, Bengali, Tamil and Simplified Chinese. We have tried to make every version say the same thing, and English is the version we use in preparing the translations. If a translation says something different, we will not rely on that difference in a way that harms you, and we will correct the translation.
- This Privacy Policy is available in the same languages. If something here is unclear, you can ask us at admin@tulussg.com to explain it in your language.

### Blind observations (before comparison)
- s3 intro: "Mga Migrant Domestic Worker" vs "Migrant Workers" (inconsistent pluralisation of the two campaign names).
- s11 Media productions: "kung may hiwalay **kang** nakasulat na pahintulot" literally reads "if **you** have separate written permission" — ambiguous about who gives permission to whom; should be "your separate written permission (to us)".
- Mixed use of "pagpayag" (consent) and "pahintulot" (permission) — needs checking against English consent/permission split.

## Comparison verdict

I compared all 79 blocks and the `ui` strings with en.json. The translation is faithful and complete. I found no omissions or additions, and every date, period and number matches: 15 Oct–15 Nov 2026, 26 Oct–26 Nov 2026, 28 Feb 2027, 31 Mar 2027, 30 days, 2 years, 12 months, five working days, about a week, about a month, UEN. The modal strength is also preserved, including "only", "may", "will", "unless the law requires or permits otherwise" and "where the law requires it". Consent terms are consistent across the policy and the forms: "pagpayag" means consent, matching the form checkbox "Pumapayag ako", and "pahintulot" means the written permission. Withdrawal terms are kept apart: "umatras" means withdrawing from a campaign and "bawiin" means withdrawing consent or permission. "Burahin" means delete. I found one consent/permission slip and one ambiguous sentence (see below).
