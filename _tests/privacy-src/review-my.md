# Burmese (my) privacy policy — independent review

## Back-translation

Literal English back-translation of my.json, written before opening en.json.

### Introduction
1. TulusSG pledges to handle personal information responsibly and respectfully.
2. TulusSG is a project that began/emerged from within the community of **Zan J Private Limited (UEN: 202616105R)**. The Share Your Story programmes are carried out by TulusSG.
3. Zan J Private Limited, which operates TulusSG, is responsible for collecting and using the personal information that you give. In this privacy policy, mentions of "we", "us" and "TulusSG" mean Zan J Private Limited acting through its projects and brands.
4. This privacy policy explains the information we may collect, the reason it is collected, how it is used, and the things you can choose.

### Section 3 — Stage 1: Registration of interest
- If you fill in the registration form, we collect the following: your name, WhatsApp number, nationality and preferred language; the story you choose to share (if any); social-media accounts you choose to share (if any); your email address only if you request a copy of your form; and your consent (together with the date and time, the version of the text you agreed to, and the language the page was shown in). We also give a reference number for your registration.
- We use this information to review what you submitted, to understand general information about participants in the programme, and to contact you about the programme you registered for. Unless you separately agree, we do not use it for marketing or for other programmes.
- Your registration can be viewed only by people TulusSG has authorised to manage the programme and by the service providers who operate our registration system (see section 6). If your story contains personal or sensitive experiences, it is handled with special care (see section 4).
- During the selection period we may talk with you by WhatsApp or phone to learn more about your story. These conversations are not recorded for publication. You do not need to sign anything to take part in them.
- Registering does not mean you will be selected. It is not permission for us to film, photograph or record you, or to publish your story or personal information.

### Section 3 — Stage 2: If you are selected
- We will explain the proposed production. Before any filming, photographing, audio or video capture, or interview intended for media production or publication, we will ask you for separate written permission. That written permission will explain how your story, name, image, voice and captured audio/video may be used. It will include how you want to be described, the places (channels) where publication is intended, whether the content may be kept with no time limit, and the arrangements for withdrawing (revoking) permission.
- Persons or organisations working on production for TulusSG will receive only the personal information reasonably needed for the work they agreed to.
- **Copy of your form.** If you request a copy, we first email a link to the address you gave, to check that it is your address. We use that address only to send the copy.

### Section 3 — Contact form
- If you write to us through the contact form on our home page, we receive your name, email address, the role you choose, and your message. We use them to reply to you and to follow up the matter you asked about (for example, volunteering or working with us). We do not put them into programme registrations, and we do not use them for marketing either. How long they are kept is explained in section 7.

### Section 6 — Service providers and processing overseas
- We use service providers to run our website, registration system and communications. They process personal information only according to our instructions.
- Service providers that store the information you give us:
  - **Google (Google Workspace and Google Cloud).** Our registration server runs on Google Cloud in Singapore. Registrations are stored in private Google Sheets, a separate one for each programme, and our emails (including a copy of your form if you request it) are sent and stored in Gmail.
  - **Google Apps Script.** Delivers messages sent through the contact form on our home page to our email.
  - **WhatsApp (Meta).** If you talk with us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp handles that conversation under its own rules.
- Service providers that only know that you visited our website: **GitHub**, which hosts the website, and **Google Fonts**, which supplies fonts. Like any website, they receive technical information such as your device's IP address. They do not receive the answers in your form. YouTube is described in section 12A.
- Some of this information may be stored or processed in the data centres of service providers outside Singapore. If so, we rely on the agreements we have made with these service providers, which include data-protection and security obligations, so that the information receives a standard of protection comparable to the protection under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act.
- We remain responsible for the personal information our service providers process for us.

### Section 9 — Your choices
You can contact us at admin@tulussg.com to:
- request access to personal information we hold about you and how it was used or disclosed in the past year;
- request correction of wrong information;
- withdraw (revoke) your participation in a programme, or ask us to stop contacting you;
- withdraw your consent to our using your personal information, or a permission you have given;
- request deletion of your personal information; or
- raise a concern about how we handle your information.

**Withdrawing (revoking) participation in a programme.** If you withdraw from a programme, we will stop communications connected with your participation. Your personal information will be handled in line with the retention and deletion arrangements in section 7.

**Withdrawing consent.** We tell you what it means for you (for example, that you will no longer be able to keep taking part), and, unless the law otherwise requires or permits, we stop collecting, using and disclosing your personal information for that purpose. Withdrawal does not affect what was already done before your request.

**Deletion.** You can ask us to delete your personal information. We will examine and act on your request in line with the applicable law and our retention policy. If information needs to be kept, we will explain what is kept and why.

If your request relates to photos, captured audio/video or published media, we will also explain how it affects any separate media agreement you signed. A media agreement does not make you lose rights you have under data-protection law.

Your registration reference number is not needed, but it helps us find your information. We may ask you to confirm who you are before acting on a request. We aim to acknowledge receipt of requests within five working days and to respond as soon as reasonably possible. If we cannot respond to an access or correction request within 30 days, we will inform you in writing when we expect to be able to respond. We normally do not charge a fee for personal-information requests. If the law allows a fee and we intend to charge one, we will tell you the amount before acting.

### Section 11 — Photos, videos and media
- **TulusSG events and activities.** Photos and videos may be taken at our events and activities to show the community's work (for example on our website and social media). You will be informed when photos or video are being taken. If you have a concern about your photo or captured audio/video, contact us at admin@tulussg.com. We will review your request and act in line with the law, any permission you gave (if any), and the circumstances of that photo or capture. If you withdraw consent we rely on, we will comply with our legal obligations, including stopping further use where the law requires.
- **Media productions such as Share Your Story.** These are different. We will film, photograph, record or interview you for media production only with your separate written permission. That permission sets out how the content may be used and how withdrawal works. The process is explained in section 3.

### Section 14 — Languages
- Our pages are written in English and translated into Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia), Filipino, Myanmar, Bengali, Tamil and Chinese (simplified characters). We try to keep the content the same in every version and use the English version when preparing translations. If a translation says something different, we will not rely on that difference in a way that disadvantages you, and we will correct the translation.
- This privacy policy is available in the same languages. If there is anything in it you do not understand, you can ask us at admin@tulussg.com to explain it in your language.

### Section 15 — Contact us
Zan J Private Limited (UEN: 202616105R), which operates TulusSG, is responsible for collecting and using the personal information described in this policy. If you have questions about this policy or want to make a request under section 9, contact our personal-information protection officer: [contact block].

## Comparison verdict

All 79 blocks compared with en.json. Overall the translation is accurate and complete. Dates (15 Oct–15 Nov 2026, 26 Oct–26 Nov 2026, 28 Feb 2027, 31 Mar 2027), periods (5 working days, 30 days, 2 years, 12 months, about a week, about a month), the UEN and section cross-references all match. The modal strength holds throughout: "may" is နိုင်, "will" is မည်, "only" is သာ, and "unless the law requires or permits otherwise" is ဥပဒေက အခြားနည်း လိုအပ်ခြင်း သို့မဟုတ် ခွင့်ပြုခြင်း မရှိပါက. "Withdraw" is translated as ရုပ်သိမ်း everywhere. နုတ်ထွက် does not appear, and neither does ထွက်. Registration is စာရင်းသွင်း throughout. Consent is သဘောတူခွင့်ပြုချက်, permission is ခွင့်ပြုချက်, agreement is သဘောတူချက်/သဘောတူညီချက်, and media agreement is မီဒီယာ သဘောတူစာချုပ်. These follow the English distinctions consistently. The Unicode scan found no Zawgyi code points, no ZWSP, no misplaced vowel-E and no duplicated marks.

## Changes made

1. **Intro para 2 (block 1), "ground-up community initiative of Zan J Private Limited".** Before: "a project that emerged from within Zan J Private Limited's community". After: "a community initiative of Zan J Private Limited that emerged from within the community". Reason: the old wording made the community belong to the company, which misstates the relationship.
2. **Section 7 table, row 1 (non-selected registrations).** Before: the "unless a matter is still open" clause came first, as an awkward "…ပါက မှလွဲ၍" construction placed ahead of the timing. After: the main rule (reviewed and deleted after selection, by 28 Feb 2027 / 31 Mar 2027) comes first, followed by "(unless a matter is still open)" in brackets. Reason: low-literacy readers could misread the old order, and the exception now reads clearly as an exception.
3. **Section 9, list item 3, "withdraw from a campaign".** Before: "revoke participation in a programme". After: the same, plus "(no longer take part)". Reason: ရုပ်သိမ်း applied to participation is formal and legalistic. The plain gloss keeps the meaning clear without using any word that suggests resigning a job.
4. **Section 9, "Withdrawing from a campaign" (block 54).** Before: "we will stop communications connected with your participation". After: "we will stop your participation and also stop related communications". Reason: the old wording left out the commitment to stop the participation itself.
5. **Section 3, Stage 2 (block 22), "whether the content may remain available indefinitely".** Before: "whether content may continue to be kept with no time limit". After: "whether content may be kept available to view with no time limit". Reason: the English refers to public availability, not internal storage.

## Residual notes

- "Campaign" is translated as အစီအစဉ် ("programme/scheme") throughout. "Programme" in sections 1, 2 and 10 is ပရိုဂရမ်, so the two terms stay distinct. This is acceptable, but readers who see the English word "campaign" on the site will not find a literal equivalent.
- Nationality is နိုင်ငံသား, the standard form-field term (literally "citizen"). It is acceptable.
- Some loanwords are glossed in brackets: spreadsheet, marketing, host, font, cookie, version history, deleted-items folder, backup. This helps readers but is wordy. Native-speaker review could cut some of them.
- "Data Protection Officer" is ကိုယ်ရေးအချက်အလက် ကာကွယ်ရေး တာဝန်ခံ. Section 15 uses it consistently with ui.dpo.
- Section 9 "Withdrawing consent" uses the present tense ရပ်ဆိုင်းပါသည်, which matches the English "we stop". No change was needed.
- A native Myanmar speaker should still do a final read for register and naturalness. This review did not find any meaning errors beyond those fixed.
