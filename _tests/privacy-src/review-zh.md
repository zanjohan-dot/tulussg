### Introduction
- TulusSG is committed to handling personal information in a responsible and respectful manner.
- TulusSG is a grassroots community initiative initiated by **Zan J Private Limited (UEN: 202616105R)**. The Share Your Story campaigns are held by TulusSG.
- Zan J Private Limited, which operates TulusSG, is responsible for collecting and using the personal information you provide. In this privacy policy, "we" and "TulusSG" both refer to Zan J Private Limited, which carries out its work through its various initiatives and brands.
- This privacy policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you can make.

### Section 3 — Share Your Story campaigns
(Lead) TulusSG holds two Share Your Story campaigns: **Share Your Story: Foreign Domestic Helpers** (registration period 15 October to 15 November 2026) and **Share Your Story: Guest Workers** (registration period 26 October to 26 November 2026). Both campaigns work the same way and are both divided into two stages. Each campaign's registration data is kept in its own separate spreadsheet.

**Stage 1: Register your interest**
- If you fill in the registration form, we will collect: your name, WhatsApp number, nationality and preferred language; the story you choose to share (if any); the social media account you choose to share (if any); your email address (only when you ask for a copy of the form); and your consent record, including the date and time of consent, the version of the text you agreed to, and the language in which the page was displayed. We also assign a number to your registration.
- We use this information to review what you submitted, to understand the overall profile of campaign participants, and to contact you about the campaign you registered for. Unless you agree otherwise, we will not use this information for marketing or other campaigns.
- Only persons authorised by TulusSG to manage that campaign, and the service providers that run our registration system, can view your registration data (see Section 6). If your story involves personal or sensitive experiences, we will handle it with particular care (see Section 4).
- During selection, we may talk with you via WhatsApp or phone to learn more about your story. These conversations will not be recorded for publication, and you do not need to sign any document to take part in the conversation.
- Registering does not mean you will definitely be selected. Registering also does not mean we may film, photograph or record you, or publish your story or personal information.

**Stage 2: If you are selected**
- We will explain to you the planned production content. Before any filming, photographing, audio recording or video recording, or any interview for media production or publication, we will separately ask for your written permission. The written permission will explain how your story, name, likeness, voice and recordings may be used, including how you wish to be identified, the planned publication channels, whether content may be kept indefinitely, and the arrangements for withdrawing permission.
- Individuals or organisations taking part in production work for TulusSG will only receive personal information reasonably necessary for their agreed work.
- **Form copy.** If you ask for a copy, we will first send a link to the email address you provided, to verify that the address belongs to you. That email address is only used to send the copy.

**Contact form**
- If you write to us through the contact form on our home page, we will receive your name, email address, the identity (role) you choose and your message. We use this information to reply to you and follow up on the matter you asked about, for example volunteering or partnering with us. We will not add this information to campaign registration data, nor use it for marketing. Section 7 explains how long we keep this information.

### Section 6 — Service providers and overseas processing
- We use service providers to run our website, registration system and communications. They will only process personal information according to our instructions.
- Service providers that keep the information you give us:
  - **Google (Google Workspace and Google Cloud).** Our registration server runs on Google Cloud in Singapore. Registration data is kept in non-public Google Sheets, one per campaign, and our email (including the form copy you request) is sent and kept via Gmail.
  - **Google Apps Script.** Forwards messages sent through our home-page contact form to our mailbox.
  - **WhatsApp (Meta).** If we talk with you on WhatsApp, the conversation is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms.
- Service providers that only see that you visited our website: **GitHub**, which hosts this website, and **Google Fonts**, which provides the website's fonts. As with any website, they receive technical information, such as your device's IP address. They do not receive the answers you fill in on forms. For YouTube, see Section 12A.
- Some of this information may be stored or processed outside Singapore, in service providers' data centres. In such cases, we rely on agreements signed with these service providers (which include data protection and security obligations) to ensure the information receives protection comparable to the standard required by Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).
- We remain responsible for personal information processed for us by service providers.

### Section 7 — How long we keep information
- We only keep personal information for the period needed for the purpose of collection, or for the period the law requires. Retention periods differ for different records. No information is deleted automatically: we review records before the dates below and delete information no longer needed.

| Record | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Share Your Story registrations not selected, and related selection notes and WhatsApp messages | Reviewed and deleted after selection is completed, the deadline being **28 February 2027** (foreign domestic helpers) and **31 March 2027** (guest workers), unless matters are still unresolved |
| Records of participants whose stories are selected | Moved into restricted participant records, and kept as described in the written agreement you sign separately |
| Signed media agreements, and related consent and withdrawal records | Kept as described in the written agreement you sign separately |
| Published stories and production records | Kept to the extent permitted by the written agreement you sign separately, but you have the right to withdraw (Section 9) |
| If you withdraw or ask us to delete your information, your working records | Deleted, generally within 30 days; we keep a brief record of your request for at most 2 years, to prove how the request was handled |
| Your email address (if you asked for a form copy) | Deleted after the copy is sent; if the link is not used, deleted about one week after the link expires |
| Copies of emails we sent about registrations | Deleted within about one month |
| Messages sent via our home-page contact form | Kept for 12 months after our last substantive exchange with you; kept longer only if your enquiry is unresolved, a collaboration arising from it is ongoing, or the law requires it |
| Technical logs in our systems (which do not contain your answers) | About 30 days |
| Information the law requires us to keep | Kept only for the period the law requires |

- Each campaign's registration spreadsheet keeps a version history, which may contain older information, including information already deleted from the current spreadsheet. Only authorised persons can access it. When that campaign's spreadsheet is deleted after the review date, its version history is deleted along with it.
- Deleted information may remain briefly in our service providers' deleted-items folders or backups before being completely purged.

### Section 9 — Your choices
You can contact us by emailing admin@tulussg.com to:
- request access to the personal information we hold about you, and how this information was used or disclosed in the past year;
- ask us to correct incorrect information;
- withdraw from a campaign, or ask us to stop contacting you;
- withdraw your consent to our use of your personal information, or withdraw a permission you once gave;
- ask us to delete your personal information; or
- raise concerns about how we handle your information.

**Withdrawing from a campaign.** If you withdraw from a campaign, we will stop your participation and related communications. Your personal information will be handled according to the retention and deletion arrangements described in Section 7.

**Withdrawing consent.** We will tell you what withdrawing consent means for you, for example that you will no longer be able to take part, and we will stop collecting, using and disclosing your personal information for that purpose, unless the law otherwise requires or permits. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out before your request.

**Deletion.** You can ask for your personal information to be deleted. We will assess and handle your request according to applicable law and our retention policy. If some information must be kept, we will explain what was kept and why.

If your request involves photos, recordings or published media content, we will also explain how the request affects any media agreement you signed separately. A media agreement does not take away the rights you have under data protection law.

You do not need to give your registration number, but giving it helps us find your information. Before handling a request, we may ask you to confirm your identity. We aim to acknowledge receipt within five working days and reply as soon as reasonably practicable. If we cannot reply to an access or correction request within 30 days, we will tell you in writing when we expect to reply. We usually do not charge for privacy-related requests; if the law allows a fee and we intend to charge it, we will tell you the amount before processing.

### Section 11 — Photos, videos and media
- **TulusSG events.** We may take photos and videos at our events to show the community's activities, for example for our website and social media. We will let you know when photographing or filming takes place. If you have any concerns about a photo or recording of you, email us at admin@tulussg.com. We will review your request and act according to the law, any permission you have given, and the specific circumstances of the photo or recording. If you withdraw the consent we rely on, we will fulfil our legal obligations, including stopping further use when the law requires.
- **Media productions, such as Share Your Story.** This kind of situation is different. Only after obtaining your separately given written permission will we film, photograph, record or interview you for a media production. That permission will set out how the content may be used and how withdrawal works. Section 3 explains the process.

## Changes made

1. **Section 2, purpose limitation (block 12)** — strength of obligation.
   Before: 如果没有适当的依据，或在需要时未取得您的进一步同意，我们不会… ("If there is no appropriate basis, OR further consent was not obtained where needed, we will not…"). This reads as requiring *both* a basis and consent.
   After: 我们不会将您的个人信息用于与收集目的有实质差异的用途，除非具有适当的依据，或在需要时已取得您的进一步同意。 ("We will not use … unless there is an appropriate basis, or further consent has been obtained where required.") This matches the English: either one is enough.
2. **Section 3, Stage 2 (block 22)**: "whether the content may remain available indefinitely".
   Before: 内容是否可能无限期保留 ("may be *kept* indefinitely", which is about storage).
   After: 内容是否可能无限期保持公开 ("may *remain publicly available* indefinitely"). The English is about the content staying published, not about keeping records.
3. **Section 6, overseas transfers (block 40)**: name of the Act.
   Before: 《个人资料保护法》. After: 《个人资料保护法令》, the Act's official Singapore Chinese title (2012年个人资料保护法令).
4. **Section 7 table, row 1**: review deadlines.
   Before: …审查并删除，期限为 2027年2月28日…和 2027年3月31日… ("period is 28 Feb / 31 Mar 2027"). A reader could take this to mean the data is *kept until* those dates.
   After: …审查并删除，最迟分别于 2027年2月28日（外籍家庭帮佣）和 2027年3月31日（客工）前完成… ("completed by 28 Feb / 31 Mar 2027 at the latest, respectively"). This matches "by".
