### Introduction
- (intro) TulusSG is committed to handling personal information with responsibility and respect.
- TulusSG is a community initiative grown from the grassroots of **Zan J Private Limited (UEN: 202616105R)**. TulusSG runs the Share Your Story campaigns.
- Zan J Private Limited, which operates TulusSG, is responsible for collecting and using the personal information you give. In this privacy policy, "we", "our" and "TulusSG" mean Zan J Private Limited, which works through its initiatives and brands.
- This privacy policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and what choices you have.

### 3. Share Your Story campaigns
- TulusSG runs two Share Your Story campaigns: **Share Your Story: Migrant Domestic Workers** (registration open from 15 October to 15 November 2026) and **Share Your Story: Migrant Workers** (registration open from 26 October to 26 November 2026). Both campaigns run in the same way, in two stages. Each campaign's registrations are kept in its own separate spreadsheet.

**Stage 1: Registering your interest**
- When you fill in the registration form, we collect: your name, WhatsApp number, nationality and preferred language; the story you want to share, if any; the social media accounts you want to share, if any; your email address, only if you want a copy of your form; and your consent, together with the date and time, the version of the text you consented to, and the language the page was shown in. We also give your registration a reference number.
- We use this information to review what you submitted, to get an overall picture of who is taking part in the campaign, and to contact you about the campaign you registered for. Unless you consent separately, we do not use it for marketing or for any other campaign.
- Your registration can be seen only by people authorised by TulusSG to run the campaign, and by the service providers that run our registration system (see section 6). If your story contains personal or sensitive experiences, we handle it with special care (see section 4).
- During selection, we may talk with you by WhatsApp or telephone to learn more about your story. These conversations are not recorded for publication, and you do not have to sign anything to take part in them.
- Registering does not mean you will be selected. Registering does not give us permission to film, photograph or record you, or to publish your story or personal information.

**Stage 2: If you are selected**
- We will explain the proposed production to you. Before any filming, photography, audio or video recording, or any interview for media production or publication, we will ask for your separate written permission. That written permission will explain how your story, name, appearance, voice and recordings may be used, including how you want to be identified, which channels publication is planned on, whether the content may stay published indefinitely, and the arrangements for withdrawing permission.
- People or organisations working on the production for TulusSG will receive only the personal information reasonably needed for the work they agreed to.
- **Copy of your form.** If you want a copy, we first email a link to the address you gave, to check that the address is yours. We use that address only to send the copy.

**Contact form**
- If you write to us through the contact form on our home page, we receive your name, email address, the role you chose and your message. We use these to reply to you and to take next steps on what you asked about, such as volunteering or working with us. We do not add them to campaign registrations or use them for marketing. How long we keep them is explained in section 7.

### 6. Service providers and processing abroad
- We use service providers to run our website, registration system and communications. They process personal information only according to our instructions.
- Service providers that hold information you give:
  - **Google (Google Workspace and Google Cloud).** Our registration server runs on Google Cloud in Singapore. Registrations are kept in private Google Sheets, a separate one for each campaign, and our emails, including your form copy if you ask, are sent and kept in Gmail.
  - **Google Apps Script.** Delivers messages sent through our home-page contact form to our email.
  - **WhatsApp (Meta).** If we talk with you on WhatsApp, WhatsApp handles the conversation under its own terms.
- Service providers that only see that you visited our website: **GitHub**, which hosts the website, and **Google Fonts**, which supplies the letter shapes (fonts). Like any other website, they receive technical information such as your device's IP address. They do not receive your form answers. YouTube is covered in section 12A.
- Some of this information may be kept or processed outside Singapore, in service providers' data centres. When this happens, to ensure the information gets protection of a standard comparable to the protection due under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (personal information protection law), we rely on our contracts with these providers, which contain data protection and security obligations.
- We remain responsible for personal information our service providers process on our behalf.

- Each campaign's registration spreadsheet has a version history, which may contain earlier information, even information removed from the current spreadsheet. Access to it is limited to authorised staff only. When a campaign's spreadsheet is deleted after its review date, the version history is also removed.
- Deleted information may remain for a short time in our service providers' deleted-items folders or backups before it is fully removed.

### 9. Your choices
- You can contact us at admin@tulussg.com for the following:
  - asking to see the personal information we hold about you, and to know how it was used or disclosed in the past one year;
  - asking to correct wrong information;
  - withdrawing yourself from a campaign, or asking us to stop contacting you;
  - withdrawing the consent given for our use of your personal information, or any permission you gave;
  - asking us to delete your personal information; or
  - raising a concern about how we handled your information.
- **Withdrawing from a campaign.** If you withdraw from a campaign, we will stop your participation and related communications. Your personal information will be handled according to the retention and deletion arrangements in section 7.
- **Withdrawing consent.** We tell you what this will mean for you, for example that you can no longer take part, and we stop collecting, using and disclosing your personal information for that purpose, unless the law requires or permits otherwise. Withdrawal does not affect what was done before your request.
- **Deletion.** You can request deletion of your personal information. We will assess your request under applicable law and our data retention policy and act accordingly. Where information must be kept, we will explain what is being kept and why.
- If your request concerns photos, recordings or published media, we will also explain how it affects any separate media agreement you signed. A media agreement does not take away your rights under data protection law.
- Your registration reference number is not needed, but it helps us find your information. Before acting on a request we may ask you to confirm your identity. We try to acknowledge receipt of a request within five working days and to reply as fast as reasonably possible. If we cannot answer a request to see or correct information within 30 days, we will tell you in writing when we expect to be able to answer. We usually do not charge a fee for privacy requests; if the law allows a fee and we want to charge it, we will tell you the amount before starting work.

### 11. Photos, videos and media
- **TulusSG events and activities.** To show what the community does, for example on our website and social media, we may take photos and videos at our events and activities. We will tell you when photography or filming is happening. If you have a concern about a photo or recording of you, please contact us at admin@tulussg.com. We will review your request and act according to the law, any permission you gave, and the circumstances of the photo or recording. If you withdraw consent we rely on, we will comply with our legal obligations, including stopping further use where the law requires.
- **Media productions, such as Share Your Story.** These are different. For media production we will film, photograph, record or interview you only with your separate written permission. That permission says how the content may be used and how withdrawal works. The process is explained in section 3.

### 14. Languages
- Our pages are written in English and translated into Bahasa Indonesia, Filipino, Burmese, Bengali, Tamil and Simplified Chinese. We have tried to say the same thing in every version, and we use the English version to produce the translations. If a translation says something different, we will not rely on that difference in a way that disadvantages you, and we will correct the translation.
- This privacy policy is available in the same languages. If anything in it seems unclear, you can ask us at admin@tulussg.com to explain it in your language.

## Changes made

1. Block 1 (Introduction). Before: "…Zan J Private Limited-এর তৃণমূল থেকে গড়ে ওঠা একটি কমিউনিটি উদ্যোগ" (can be read as "an initiative grown from the grassroots *of* Zan J Private Limited"). After: "…Zan J Private Limited-এর একটি কমিউনিটি উদ্যোগ, যা তৃণমূল পর্যায় থেকে গড়ে উঠেছে" ("a community initiative of Zan J Private Limited, which grew from the grassroots"). Reason: the old word order attached "grassroots" to the company and blurred who owns the initiative.
2. Block 53, first item (section 9, access right). Before: "কীভাবে ব্যবহার বা প্রকাশ করা হয়েছে" (could be read as "used or *published*"). After: "কীভাবে ব্যবহার করা হয়েছে বা অন্য কারও কাছে প্রকাশ করা হয়েছে" ("used, or disclosed to anyone else"). Reason: in this policy প্রকাশ also means "publish" (blocks 20, 22, 34, 64). Without the change, a reader could take the right to be "how it was published" and miss disclosure to third parties.
3. Block 55 (section 9, withdrawing consent). Before: "সংগ্রহ, ব্যবহার ও প্রকাশ করা বন্ধ করি" → After: "সংগ্রহ, ব্যবহার ও অন্য কারও কাছে প্রকাশ করা বন্ধ করি" ("stop collecting, using and disclosing to anyone else"). Reason: same publish/disclose ambiguity as change 2, here in a binding commitment.
4. Section 7 table, contact-form row. Before: "মূল বিষয়ে আমাদের সর্বশেষ যোগাযোগের পর ১২ মাস" ("12 months after our last contact *on the main matter*"). After: "আমাদের সর্বশেষ প্রকৃত (বিষয়ভিত্তিক) যোগাযোগের পর ১২ মাস" ("12 months after our last real (on-topic) exchange"). Reason: English says "substantive exchange", meaning a real exchange rather than an automatic reply. "On the main matter" narrowed it to one topic and could change when the 12 months start.

## Residual notes

- Block 48 still uses প্রকাশ for "disclosure" in the security list (unauthorised access, misuse, loss, disclosure…). This was left as is because the list of harms makes the meaning clear.
- Block 1 keeps the English "ক্যাম্পেইন" and "Share Your Story". These are familiar to the readers and match the campaign pages.
- English itself uses both "separate written permission" (Stage 2, section 11) and "separate written agreement" (section 7 table, section 9). Bengali follows it with অনুমতি / চুক্তি. If the English is ever made consistent, update the Bengali to match.
- "১২A" mixes a Bengali digit with a Latin letter. The id is fixed, so this is fine.
- The register is clear and fairly plain. A few long sentences remain (block 22, block 40, the contact-form retention row). They are accurate, but a native-speaker reading test with the target readers would be useful before launch.
