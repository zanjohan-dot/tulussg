About the carnival

The carnival will introduce participants to five sports commonly played in Singapore through beginner-friendly activity stations.

How it works

  • Spend 25 minutes at each station, learning basic skills and trying fun challenges.
  • Take a 5-minute break, then move on to the next sport.
  • No prior sporting experience is required.
