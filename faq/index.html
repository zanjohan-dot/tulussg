What is TulusSG?

A ground-up community initiative for migrant domestic workers in Singapore. We bring people together through sport, activities and friendship.

Who can join?

Migrant domestic workers in Singapore. Employers, volunteers, coaches and organisations are welcome to get in touch too.

What activities are planned?

We're exploring kayaking, football, floorball, pickleball, badminton, basketball, netball, piloxing, DRUMS UP and singing. Nothing is confirmed yet. We share dates in our WhatsApp community.

What is the 1,000 goal?

We're working towards a community of 1,000 migrant domestic workers. When we reach it, we hope to organise a TulusSG Sports Carnival.

How do I join?

Join our WhatsApp community. For news only, follow our WhatsApp Channel. You can also email admin@tulussg.com.
