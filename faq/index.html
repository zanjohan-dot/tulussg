What is TulusSG?

A community initiative serving migrant communities in Singapore: migrant domestic workers and migrant workers. We focus on widening access to meaningful activities, creating space for their stories, and connecting them with relevant community support.

Who can join?

Migrant domestic workers and migrant workers in Singapore. Each community has its own channels and activities. Employers, volunteers, coaches and organisations are welcome to get in touch too.

What activities are planned?

For domestic workers, we're exploring kayaking, football, floorball, pickleball, badminton, basketball, netball, piloxing, DRUMS UP and singing. For migrant workers, we're planning sport and recreation activities. Nothing is confirmed yet. Each announcement will say who can take part.

When is the TulusSG Sports Carnival?

Sunday, 10 January 2027. The venue, time and registration will be announced on the Sports Carnival page.

Who are activities for?

Each activity is planned for a specific community. Its announcement will say who can take part.

What is the 1,000 goal?

Our domestic worker community is working towards 1,000 members. It is an outreach goal for our WhatsApp community.

Does TulusSG provide support services?

We help people find relevant organisations, resources and programmes, and refer them where appropriate. We don't provide every service ourselves.

How do I join?

Domestic workers can join our WhatsApp community. Migrant workers can follow our WhatsApp Channel for news. Anyone can email admin@tulussg.com.
