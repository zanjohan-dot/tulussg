TulusSG Singapore

Our Community

TulusSG is a community initiative serving migrant communities in Singapore: migrant domestic workers and migrant workers. We focus on widening access to meaningful activities, creating space for their stories to be heard, and connecting them with relevant community support.

Meaningful activities

We widen access to recreational, sporting, learning and community activities, through our own initiatives and with partners. For domestic workers, kayaking, football, floorball, pickleball, badminton, basketball, netball, piloxing, DRUMS UP and singing are being explored. For migrant workers, sport and recreation activities are being planned. Nothing is confirmed until we announce it.

Their stories

We create space for people to share their experiences and perspectives. Our current campaign, Share Your Story, is for domestic helpers.

Community support

We help people find relevant organisations, resources and programmes, and refer them where appropriate. We don't provide every service ourselves.

Our domestic worker community goal

Our domestic worker community is working towards 1,000 members. When we get there, we hope to hold a TulusSG Sports Carnival.
