# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [date of publication]**

> This policy is currently available in English. Reviewed translations in Bahasa Indonesia, Filipino and Burmese will be added. Each TulusSG form also has a short privacy notice in your language.

## 1. Who we are

TulusSG is a community initiative of **Zan J Private Limited** (UEN [●]), a company registered in Singapore.

In this policy, "TulusSG", "we", "us" and "our" mean Zan J Private Limited acting through TulusSG. Zan J Private Limited is the organisation responsible for personal data collected through the TulusSG website, campaigns and activities.

This policy explains:
* what personal data we collect;
* why we collect it;
* who can see it;
* how long we keep it;
* how you can ask about it, correct it, or withdraw your consent.

We follow Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

## 2. How this policy works with our forms

Each TulusSG campaign or activity form includes a short notice. That notice tells you:
* what the form collects;
* why it collects it;
* who will see it.

This policy gives the fuller picture and applies to all our forms. If a form's notice is more specific than this policy, the form's notice applies to that form.

## 3. Personal data we collect

**Information you give us through a form.** This depends on the form. It may include:
* the name you would like us to use;
* your mobile or WhatsApp number;
* your preferred language;
* your answers to the questions on that form;
* anything you choose to write to us, such as your story.

Each form shows which fields are required and which are optional.

**Messages you send us.** This includes WhatsApp messages, emails and anything you send when you ask us a question or make a request.

**Information about your participation.** If you are selected for or take part in an activity, we may keep:
* records of our contact with you;
* your participation;
* any separate consents you give, such as consent to be filmed.

**Technical information.** When you visit our website:
* **Web hosting.** Our website is hosted by GitHub, and our forms are sent to a server run by Render. These providers record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you requested, in their system logs. They use it to deliver the service and keep it secure.
* **Abuse prevention.** When you submit a form, our server uses your IP address for about 15 minutes to limit repeated submissions. We do not store it with your registration.
* **Fonts.** [DECISION: if fonts are self-hosted, delete this point.] Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Google therefore receives your IP address when you visit.
* **Language setting.** Your browser remembers the language you chose on our site (for example, "Bahasa Indonesia"). This setting is stored only on your device and does not identify you.
* **No analytics or advertising.** We do not use analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels. If this changes, we will update this policy first.

**What we do not ask for.** Our forms do not ask for your FIN, passport or work permit number, bank details, home address, salary, or your employer's name.

## 4. Why we use your personal data

We use your personal data only for these purposes:
* to consider your registration or expression of interest for the campaign or activity you chose;
* to contact you about that campaign or activity;
* to organise your participation if you are selected;
* to reply to your questions and requests;
* to keep our website, forms and records secure, and prevent misuse;
* to keep records needed to show what you agreed to, such as your consent;
* to meet our legal obligations.

We will not use your personal data for another purpose unless:
* we tell you and ask for your consent; or
* the law allows it.

For example, registering for one campaign does not mean we will contact you about other campaigns. If we would like to, we will ask you separately.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for advertising.

## 5. Share Your Story

This section describes the Share Your Story form, found at tulussg.com/campaigns/share-your-story.

**What the form collects:**
* the name you would like us to use;
* your WhatsApp number;
* [DECISION: "your nationality (optional)" or remove];
* your preferred language;
* your story (optional, up to 1,000 characters);
* whether you might be comfortable being filmed;
* your consent to be contacted.

We also record the date and time you registered and the version of the consent wording you saw.

**What it is used for:** to consider you for the Share Your Story media project, and to contact you about it.

**Registering is not permission to publish.** Submitting the form only tells us you are interested. It does **not** give us permission to film, photograph or record you, or to publish or share your story, image, voice or personal data.

If you are selected, we will explain the project to you in your language before anything happens. We will then ask for your consent separately for each of these:
1. taking part;
2. being filmed, photographed or recorded;
3. publishing or using the result.

You may decide not to take part at any stage before you give these consents.

**Who it is for:** [DECISION: "This project is for adults aged 18 and over who work as domestic helpers in Singapore."]

## 6. If you share a personal story

Please only share what you are comfortable telling us. Please **do not include**:
* identification numbers (FIN, passport or work permit);
* bank or financial details;
* your address;
* medical or health information;
* names or identifying details of your employer, their family, the people you care for, or other people;
* your employer's private or household information.

If you include these details, we will:
* keep them confidential;
* use them only to consider your registration;
* delete or remove details we do not need.

We will not publish anything about you, or about anyone you mention, from your registration.

## 7. Who can see your personal data

**The TulusSG team.** Only named team members who work on the relevant campaign can see your registration. Each campaign's registrations are stored separately, and access is restricted to those people.

**Service providers.** We use service providers to run our website, forms, storage and messages. They may handle your personal data only to provide their service to us:

| Provider | What it does for us |
|---|---|
| Google (Google Workspace / Google Cloud) [CONFIRM] | stores campaign registrations in private spreadsheets |
| Render | runs the server that receives form submissions (Singapore region) |
| [● email provider for admin@tulussg.com] | internal email notifications |
| GitHub | hosts the public website |
| Google Fonts [DECISION: delete if self-hosted] | provides the fonts on our pages |

**WhatsApp.** If we contact you on WhatsApp, your messages are also handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under WhatsApp's own terms. We do not give WhatsApp your registration details.

**Others.** We share personal data with others only:
* with your consent. For example, we may introduce you to a production partner if you are selected, and we will tell you who they are first; or
* where the law requires or allows it. For example, to professional advisers or authorities.

## 8. Personal data outside Singapore

Some of our service providers may store or process personal data outside Singapore. When this happens, we take steps to make sure your personal data receives protection comparable to the PDPA. This includes using providers bound by data processing terms. [CONFIRM: true only after the vendor review in Annex B, items M7–M8.]

## 9. How long we keep personal data

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for, or for legal or business reasons.

For Share Your Story [DECISION: periods below as recommended]:

| Situation | How long we keep it |
|---|---|
| You are not selected | we delete your registration within 6 months after registration closes |
| You ask us to stop, or to delete your registration | we delete it within 30 days. We keep only a short note of your request, so that we do not contact you again. |
| You are selected | we keep it during the production and for up to 12 months after the project is published or ends |
| Signed consent and release forms | we keep them for as long as the content is used, and for a limited period afterwards in case of questions or disputes |

We may keep information that no longer identifies anyone, such as the number of people who registered.

## 10. How we protect personal data

We use reasonable security arrangements, including:
* a separate private spreadsheet for each campaign, with access limited to named people;
* passwords and credentials that are never included in our website code;
* encrypted connections (HTTPS);
* limits on repeated or automated submissions.

No website or storage system can be completely secure. We cannot guarantee that personal data will never be lost or accessed without permission.

**Data breaches.** If we become aware of a data breach, we will:
* assess it promptly;
* take steps to contain it;
* notify the Personal Data Protection Commission and the affected people where the PDPA requires.

## 11. Your choices and rights

You can contact our Data Protection Officer (section 15) to:
* **ask for access.** You can ask what personal data we hold about you, and how it has been used or disclosed in the past year.
* **correct your data.** You can ask us to correct personal data that is wrong or incomplete.
* **withdraw your consent.** You can ask us to stop contacting you, or to stop considering you for a campaign.
* **ask a question** or make a complaint about how we handled your personal data.

**How to ask:**
* You can write in English, Bahasa Indonesia, Filipino or Burmese.
* You can use WhatsApp or email.
* We may need to confirm who you are before we share or change anything. For example, we may reply to the number you registered with.

**When we reply:**
* We will reply within 30 days. If we need longer, we will tell you within 30 days when we will reply.
* For access requests, we may charge a reasonable fee. If so, we will tell you the amount before we proceed. [DECISION: or "We do not charge for requests."]
* In some cases the law allows or requires us to refuse a request. If so, we will explain why, where we are allowed to.

**Withdrawing consent:**
* We will act on your request within 10 working days.
* After that, we will stop contacting you about that campaign and will no longer consider you for it.
* Withdrawing does not affect anything we did before your request.
* If you have signed a separate release for published content, that release explains what happens if you change your mind after publication.

**Deletion.** You can also ask us to delete your registration. We will do so unless we need to keep something for a legal reason. If we do, we will tell you what we are keeping and why.

## 12. Children and young people

Our current campaign forms are for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through them.

If a future activity is for young people, its form will say so. It will also explain any parent or guardian consent needed.

If you believe a child's personal data was sent to us through an adult form, please contact us, and we will delete it.

## 13. Photos, videos and media

Taking part in a TulusSG activity does not by itself give us permission to use your image, voice or story.

Before any filming, photography, recording or publication:
* we will explain what is involved;
* we will ask for your consent separately, in writing.

Where possible, we will explain this in your language.

## 14. Links, changes and language

**Links.** Our website may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

**Changes.** We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date each time. If a change significantly affects how we use personal data we already hold, we will tell the people affected, and ask for consent where needed.

**Language.** If a translation of this policy differs from the English version, the English version applies. However, we will interpret any difference fairly and in your favour where reasonable. [DECISION: governing-language wording to be confirmed by ALAC.]
